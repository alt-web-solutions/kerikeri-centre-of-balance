Abdominal Acupuncture (Fu Zhen)

Abdominal acupuncture (AA) is a revolutionary new acupuncture technique, It is very gentle, there is little needle sensation, for this reason, most people prefer it to traditional acupuncture. It gives great results it can be used to treat any condition that traditional acupuncture treats (usually with a faster curative time). Results are long-lasting and are often felt instantaneously hence abdominal is also known as the “miracle therapy”.
